1. Product Overview
Codenter AI SDR is an autonomous sales-development platform that learns a company's business from its website and connected business data, imports leads from a CRM, creates highly contextual outbound email campaigns, manages follow-ups, understands replies, handles common sales conversations, qualifies prospects, and books meetings.
The product should be built as an AI sales workflow system rather than a simple bulk-email tool. The core loop is: understand the business → understand the target account → generate a relevant message → send on schedule → detect and interpret replies → respond safely → qualify intent → book a meeting or hand off → synchronize the outcome back to the CRM.
CORE POSITIONINGEnter your website, connect your CRM, define your target. Codenter becomes an AI SDR that researches, personalizes, follows up, handles replies and books meetings.
1.1 Product Goals
Turn a website URL into a structured business knowledge base and reusable AI context.
Connect to a CRM and import eligible leads/accounts with field-level mapping.
Let users define campaigns using ICP, objective, messaging strategy, timing and safety rules.
Generate personalized outbound emails using business knowledge + lead/account context + campaign goals.
Execute multi-step follow-up sequences automatically with stop conditions and business-hour rules.
Monitor inbound replies and classify intent before deciding whether AI should respond.
Answer business questions using grounded knowledge instead of inventing pricing, capabilities or policies.
Qualify interested prospects, capture qualification signals and update CRM fields.
Offer calendar availability and book meetings with explicit confirmation.
Provide transparent logs, analytics, controls and human handoff at every critical point.
1.2 Non-Goals for the First Production Release
Do not build a generic consumer email client.
Do not position the system as an unrestricted mass-spam sender.
Do not let the AI invent product claims, discounts, legal terms or availability.
Do not auto-send every AI-generated reply without policy controls and confidence thresholds.
Do not support every CRM/email/calendar provider in V1; use an adapter architecture and launch with a focused set.

2. End-to-End User Journey
Create workspace and verify the business identity.
Enter company website URL.
Run Website Intelligence scan: crawl pages, render important pages when required, extract business facts, products, services, FAQs, pricing signals, CTAs, industries, brand voice and brand constraints.
Review the generated Business Profile and correct anything inaccurate.
Connect CRM and choose which leads/accounts can be synchronized.
Connect a sending mailbox and complete domain/email readiness checks.
Connect a calendar provider if meeting booking is enabled.
Define ICP and campaign objective.
Select leads or import a filtered lead segment.
Generate campaign steps and preview sample emails.
Launch campaign. Scheduler releases eligible steps into the sending queue.
Inbound email worker receives replies and attaches them to the correct thread/lead.
Conversation Engine classifies reply intent, checks knowledge and campaign state, then chooses: ignore/stop, send AI reply, ask clarification, schedule follow-up, or hand off.
If meeting intent is detected, the agent offers approved slots and books only after confirmation.
Campaign and CRM states are updated in near real time.
Dashboard exposes sent, delivered, replied, positive replies, meetings, bounces, unsubscribes, AI actions and exceptions.

4. Website Intelligence & Business Training
The AI SDR should not be trained only from raw website text. The website is the authoritative starting point for business knowledge, while CRM fields, user-defined settings and campaign configuration provide operational context.
4.1 Crawl Pipeline
Validate URL and normalize domain.
Fetch robots.txt and apply crawl policy.
Discover sitemap.xml and internal links.
Prioritize high-value pages: home, product/service, pricing, about, industries, use cases, case studies, FAQ, contact, documentation and legal/policy pages.
Render JavaScript-heavy pages with a browser worker when static HTTP extraction is insufficient.
Capture clean text plus source URL, title, headings, timestamps and extraction method.
Optionally capture screenshots of representative pages for visual/brand analysis.
Remove navigation/footer duplication and normalize content.
Classify page type and business topic.
Extract structured facts with provenance.
Chunk useful content and generate embeddings.
Build/update Business Profile and Knowledge Base.
Run quality checks and surface low-confidence fields for human review.

4.3 Knowledge Rules
Every factual knowledge item should retain source URL and extraction timestamp.
Knowledge should be versioned so AI decisions can be reproduced.
Deleted or stale pages must not silently remain authoritative forever.
Pricing and policy information should have freshness metadata.
When retrieval returns conflicting facts, prefer the newest verified source or request human review.
The model must not treat lead-specific data as company-wide truth.

7. Personalized Email Generation
The generation pipeline should assemble a bounded context package before calling the model. Do not dump the entire knowledge base into the prompt.
7.1 Context Assembly
Load verified Business Profile.
Load campaign objective, offer, tone and sequence step.
Load canonical lead and company data.
Retrieve only relevant knowledge chunks.
Add approved personalization signals and research facts.
Add previous thread summary if replying/following up.
Apply prohibited-claim and privacy filters.
Generate structured draft.
Run validation and policy checks.
Store prompt/version metadata and generated output for auditability.
7.2 Output Contract
The model should return structured JSON internally, for example: subject, body, personalization_facts, CTA, claims_used, confidence, risk_flags, and recommended_action. The API should never rely on parsing free-form model text to determine workflow state.
7.3 Personalization Rules
Use only facts available from approved sources.
Never fabricate company initiatives, recent events, technologies, budgets or personal achievements.
Personalization should be relevant to the offer, not merely decorative.
Keep cold outbound concise; avoid long AI-generated essays.
Use the recipient's role to adapt pain points and language.
Do not expose hidden enrichment data that should not be disclosed to the recipient.
Do not claim a human personally researched the prospect if the action was automated.

8.2 Reply Processing Pipeline
Receive inbound event/webhook or poll mailbox.
Normalize MIME content, strip quoted history where possible, preserve raw message securely.
Match message to email thread and lead.
Deduplicate using provider message ID + content hash.
Run unsubscribe/suppression detection first.
Classify intent and urgency.
Retrieve relevant business knowledge if a factual answer is required.
Evaluate campaign and AI policy.
Generate a structured response plan.
Run safety/claim/PII/policy validation.
Auto-send only when policy and confidence permit.
Otherwise create human handoff.
Update thread, campaign state, CRM and analytics.
Record complete audit event.
8.3 Conversation Memory
Store raw messages separately from derived summaries.
Maintain a compact rolling thread summary.
Track commitments: promised info, requested date, proposed meeting, unresolved question.
Never let a stale summary override a newer message.
Use message timestamps and sender identity to establish chronology.

10. Meeting Booking
10.1 Booking Flow
Prospect expresses meeting intent or campaign CTA requests a meeting.
AI confirms what meeting type is appropriate.
Calendar Service retrieves available slots within configured business rules.
Agent proposes a small number of slots using the prospect's timezone where reliable.
Prospect selects a slot.
System creates calendar event and records provider event ID.
Send confirmation email.
Stop relevant campaign sequence.
Update CRM: meeting booked, timestamp, meeting type, owner and event reference.
10.2 Calendar Safety
Never claim a meeting is booked until provider confirmation is successful.
Handle race conditions where two prospects attempt the same slot.
Respect calendar busy/free data and configured buffers.
Use idempotency keys for booking requests.
Do not expose private calendar event details.
11. Email Sending & Inbound Infrastructure
11.1 Provider Abstraction
Create a common interface such as EmailProvider with methods for OAuth, send, get message, list messages, watch/webhook registration, thread lookup, labels/folders and provider health. Provider-specific logic stays behind adapters.
11.2 Sending Pipeline
Campaign Scheduler creates a send job only when all eligibility checks pass.
Send worker acquires an idempotency key and lead lock.
Validate suppression, campaign state, mailbox limits and duplicate-send rules.
Render final email from approved content.
Send through provider adapter.
Persist provider message ID/thread ID and timestamp.
Schedule next step only after successful send.
Emit analytics event.
11.3 Deliverability Controls
Per-mailbox and per-domain sending caps.
Randomized pacing within configured windows; avoid burst sending.
SPF/DKIM/DMARC readiness guidance.
Bounce classification and automatic suppression.
Unsubscribe detection and global/workspace suppression lists.
Duplicate recipient prevention.
Provider rate-limit handling with exponential backoff.
Mailbox health indicators.
Campaign-level pause switch.
No sending when mailbox authentication is invalid.
Honor applicable anti-spam, privacy and platform/provider policies.
CRITICAL PRODUCT RULEThe system must make it easy to stop sending. A single unsubscribe, hard bounce, manual pause or compliance block must be able to prevent future campaign actions immediately.

12.1 Tenant Isolation
Every business object must have a workspace_id or an enforceable parent relationship to a workspace.
Never trust workspace_id from the browser; derive authorization from the authenticated membership.
Apply repository-level tenant filters and consider PostgreSQL row-level security for high-assurance deployments.
Background workers must carry explicit workspace context.

14.1 Scheduler Design
Store next_action_at in UTC; preserve lead and workspace timezone separately.
Evaluate business hours at execution time, not only at campaign creation.

Claim jobs atomically before sending.
Do not use a simple in-process Python scheduler for production-critical sends.
Support pause/resume without losing sequence state.

18. Security, Privacy & Compliance
OAuth tokens and provider secrets must be encrypted at rest.
Never expose access/refresh tokens to the frontend.
Use short-lived application sessions/access tokens.
Apply RBAC at workspace and resource level.
Validate all webhook signatures.
Encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest.
Keep audit logs for integration changes, sends, AI decisions, suppression actions and admin operations.
Implement data retention and deletion controls.
Allow workspace export/deletion workflows.
Protect inbound email HTML against active content when rendered in the UI.
Sanitize attachments and never execute unknown content.
Apply rate limits to authentication, scans, AI endpoints, webhook retries and manual send actions.
Follow applicable email marketing, privacy and provider rules for the markets in which the product is used.

25. MVP Acceptance Criteria
A user can create a workspace and securely connect supported providers.
A website URL can be scanned and converted into a structured Business Profile and searchable knowledge base.
A user can review/edit important business facts before campaign launch.
CRM leads can be imported, normalized, deduplicated and filtered.
An ICP can be defined and applied to lead scoring.
A user can create a multi-step email campaign and preview generated emails.
Campaign sends respect scheduling, business hours, suppression and rate limits.
Replies are received, threaded and classified.
Unsubscribe requests immediately suppress future outreach.
AI replies use grounded business knowledge and do not fabricate unsupported claims.
Low-confidence or sensitive cases can be routed to human handoff.
The system can qualify a prospect and store explainable qualification data.
A supported calendar can provide slots and confirm bookings.
Booked meetings stop the relevant automated sequence.
CRM state is updated after important outcomes.
All critical automated actions are auditable.
Workers and webhooks are idempotent and recover safely from retries.
The product can be paused globally and per campaign.

30. Engineering Principles
Deterministic rules control safety-critical decisions; AI assists within those boundaries.
Every external integration is behind an adapter.
Every automated action is idempotent.
Every important AI decision is explainable through structured outputs and source provenance.
Every tenant is isolated by authorization and data boundaries.
Every campaign can be paused.
Every unsubscribe is authoritative.
Every factual business claim should be traceable to a source or user-approved configuration.
Long-running work belongs in background jobs.
The database is the source of truth; queues are execution mechanisms, not state stores.
Do not optimize for maximum email volume; optimize for relevant conversations and legitimate sales outcomes.
FINAL PRODUCT DEFINITIONCodenter AI SDR is not an email automation screen with an LLM attached. It is a controlled autonomous sales system: Business Intelligence + Lead Intelligence + Campaign Orchestration + Conversation AI + Calendar + CRM synchronization.
